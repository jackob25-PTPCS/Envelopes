# Disclaimer

## Not financial advice

Envelopes is a bookkeeping tool. It arranges numbers you give it and numbers
your bank reports. It does not know your circumstances and nothing it displays
is financial, tax, legal, or investment advice.

The default milestones follow a debt-payoff sequence popularized by Dave Ramsey.
That sequence is one opinion among several — the app itself notes where the
debt snowball costs more in interest than paying highest-rate-first. Edit or
replace the milestones with whatever actually fits your situation. This project
is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Ramsey Solutions.

Talk to a qualified professional before making consequential financial
decisions.

## Your data is your responsibility

Everything lives in `DATA_DIR` on your own server. There is no cloud backup, no
recovery service, and no undo inside the app. If you delete it, it's gone. Back
it up yourself.

If you host for other people, understand that you can read their financial data
and that you become responsible for protecting it.

## Bank connectivity

Bank sync is provided by [SimpleFIN](https://beta-bridge.simplefin.org), an
independent third party with its own terms, pricing, and privacy policy. This
project is not affiliated with SimpleFIN. Access is read-only — nothing here can
move money — but coverage, accuracy, and availability are theirs, not ours.

## Unsigned binaries

The desktop and Android builds are not code-signed. Windows SmartScreen will
warn you; Android will ask you to permit installs from an unknown source. That
is expected for independent software, and it also means you are trusting
whoever built the file you downloaded. Building from source yourself is always
the safer option.
